Get hands-on training in FloodAdapt for a system in your coastal location.

Standard training packages include:
  • A fit-for-training FloodAdapt system based on open data for your coastal area of interest*
  • 8 hours of live online training for up to 15 participants, including:
    • Lessons on translating community questions into FloodAdapt input
    • Hands on exercises to:
      • Specify weather events
      • Draw in different adaptation measures
      • Consider future conditions
      • Build and run scenarios
  • Guidance in exploring FloodAdapt maps and infographics
  • Guidance on setting up and running a cost-benefit analysis in FloodAdapt
  • Overview how FloodAdapt can support adaptive planning

 

Benefits of a training package:
  • Help build capacity and system understanding
  • The FloodAdapt system is yours to keep and can be used for early-phase exploratory planning, communication, and capacity building
  • Improvement packages can transform your training system into a tailored and validated FloodAdapt system ready for use in adaptation planning and emergency preparedness.

* For areas outside the US, (the location of) a digital elevation map (DEM) will need to be provided to the FloodAdapt team for your area of interest, with a horizontal resolution of at least 10 meters.
